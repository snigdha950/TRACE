Claim boundaries
  • Prototype severity only, not official IMD/NCMRWF warning; every output requires human review and cannot self-authorize public release.
  • GEFSv12 substitute; direct NEPS-G validation missing.
  • Anomaly core is not exact cyclone center.
  • 5 km ring is a UI locator only, not modeled hazard radius or Stage-2 output.
  • Stage-2 HRRR benchmark is controlled reconstruction, not same-event BULBUL downscaling.
  • Physics-aware constraints and diagnostics tested; full thermodynamic/PDE conservation not demonstrated.
